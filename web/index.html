Which parameters work, and is the best one luck?

Every combination of one or two parameters is backtested on the same bars. The map shows where the strategy works; the box beside it measures how much of the winner's edge a search this wide would find by chance alone.

Is the winner real?

A sweep picks its best combination with hindsight. The deflated Sharpe ratio and the probability of backtest overfitting measure how much of that edge luck can explain.

What luck could have done

Blocks of the backtest's own daily returns are drawn at random, a thousand times over, keeping calm and turbulent stretches together. The spread of the paths is the range of outcomes the same edge allows.
